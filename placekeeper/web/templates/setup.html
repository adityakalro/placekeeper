Automatic progress sync

Sync progress for books that already have maps. Use Find new books on the Sync page to discover and map new titles.

Health, alerts, and retries

Health is checked every minute. Alerts appear on the Sync page and remain in history. No external notifications are sent.

Later retries double the delay, capped at 60 minutes. Uncertain writes require review and are never retried automatically.

Send to Kindle email

Uploading never sends email. Press Send to Kindle beside a library book to send it. Add the sender below to Amazon’s Approved Personal Document Email List and enable Personal Document Archiving for library discovery. Delivery is limited to 20 MiB EPUBs.
